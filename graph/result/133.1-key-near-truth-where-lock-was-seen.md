Post hoc (the split was computed after the run): on the 2,003 pulls where the lock had been seen earlier in the episode, the network key scores 93.2% (91.8 to 94.1 over 3 seeds) against 95.3% with the true values and 89.4% for the prior alone, at 400 episodes. Where memory can answer at all, it nearly does.
