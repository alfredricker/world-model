On warm touches (every touch of a child other than a cold one) the current recall closes 64% (rewired child) to 100% (default child) of the gap from the base rate to the best possible answer, mean of three worlds. On the lock-and-drawer case (the random child) it closes 90, 92 and 95%, against a bar of 60%. Slow drift closes 82%, fast drift 73%, timer 85%, chance 72%, two 76%. What the memory has within an episode it reads well; what it lacks is the next episode's start, as the cold scores show.
