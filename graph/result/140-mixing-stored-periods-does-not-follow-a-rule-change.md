The answers of stored periods of 80 episodes, each weighted by how well it predicted the object's last 20 touches, move warm touches of the rewired child (the child whose parent switch is swapped every 300 episodes) from 62.4% of the gap closed under [card 138's answer](138-item-weights-win-back-the-cost-of-old-records.md) to 63.6%. Half of the way to the reference would be 67.4%, so the first bar is missed. The other bar on harm is missed too: six other warm factors lose 2 to 6 points, drift slow 90.5% to 84.8%, drift fast 82.4% to 76.3% and chance 75.2% to 69.1% (1 point was allowed). The card explains that over 20 touches a thin period is right by luck often enough to be believed, so the answer that uses the whole store holds only 57 to 70% of the weight on children whose outcomes are partly chance. The card's own gate also missed by 0.1 point: the reference closes 72.3% against 62.4% for card 138's answer, 10 points were asked, so by its rule the card is void; the arm is reported all the same. The weight is an estimate.
