From [card 153.1](../../experiments/153.1-dynamics-classes-from-appearance-and-transitions/card.md): up to 8 classes for 4 true ones at 100 and 200 sentences, a fifth class of 10 words in 1 world of 10 at 1,000 sentences, a class of one object in 2 of 20 sprite worlds at 300 episodes. The four worlds with more than four classes at 100 sentences are the four lowest (73.0 to 76.0% of the gap). Card 153's prior on the grouping gave exactly 3 and 4 classes with enough data. Also: for known rule-breaking words at 100 sentences the posterior costs 1.413 nats against 1.312 for one class, because a stronger appearance prior outweighs one or two transitions.
