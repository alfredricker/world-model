Without the "that touch changed it" flag the 464 pulls of the middle group score 70.0 balanced accuracy; with it they score 90.3. A pull that moved nothing and a pull that moved the drawer 1 cm are close as distances and opposite as events, so the absence of an effect has to be stored as a value of its own. The flag matters in the middle group only: the lock-touched group scores 95.8 without it and 95.3 with it. The weight is an estimate. Numbers are in [results.json](../../experiments/134.1-recall-keyed-per-object/results.json); see [card 134.1](../../experiments/134.1-recall-keyed-per-object/card.md).
