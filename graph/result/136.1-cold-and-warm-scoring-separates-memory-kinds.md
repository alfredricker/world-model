Scored apart, cold touches (neither the child nor its parents seen yet in the episode) put the fixed-start child and the timer child at 100% of the gap closed and the carry child at -4%, and the rewired child at -20%. The best and worst differ by over 100 points, against a bar of 40. The score is also steady: 12 of the 13 entries with room in all three worlds are within 20 points between worlds, against a bar of four fifths; the one outside is the rewired child, cold (35 points, on about 130 touches a world). Card 136 scored all touches together and its factors were only 36 points apart. The value is the best minus the worst, rounded down from 104.
