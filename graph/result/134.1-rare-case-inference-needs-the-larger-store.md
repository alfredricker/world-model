Reported without a bar. With a store of 100 episodes the middle group scores 58.4 balanced accuracy, against 48.4 for the flat key and 90.0 for the true lock. About 9 stored pulls decide it, and with so few the choice of records is also less steady: in one draw it adds the other lock for the drawer. The other groups hold: lock touched 87.9 with the flag (flat 81.1, true lock 92.7) and reopenings 92.7. The weight is an estimate.
