Card 134.1's recall, with nothing changed, finds the true parent of every child in all three worlds among 16 candidates. On the short-term factor (the random child, the lock and drawer of cards 134 and 134.1) it closes 99, 94 and 92% of the gap from the base rate to the best possible answer, against a bar of 60%. Log loss is 0.126 for recall, 0.694 for the base rate and 0.097 for the best possible answer. The weight is an estimate.
