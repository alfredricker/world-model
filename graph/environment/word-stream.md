A synthetic stream of words built on 2026-11-20 ([card 153](../../experiments/153-kinds-found-by-one-energy-in-two-domains/card.md), `tools/card153/lang.py`) to test a mechanism outside vision. Twelve function words and content words of four classes (singular noun, plural noun, verb, adjective) in sentences from a small grammar. A word's form has a suffix and an onset; the class goes with one of them, which one is drawn per family of worlds. 100 words per class with Zipf frequencies; five frequent words of each class have the form of another class. A record is one occurrence of a word: the token two back, the token before and the token after. Word boundaries and which words are function words are given. A test bed for one component; whole cards are still judged on OGBench.
