DeepMind's multi-object Tetrominoes: 60,000 images of 32 by 32 pixels, each with three coloured tetrominoes on black and a true mask per object. Used from 2026-11-19 ([card 146](../../experiments/146-grouping-as-settling-on-a-known-benchmark/card.md)) as the first step of a test ladder for binding, because AKOrN publishes numbers on it. It has no actions and no time; it is a test bed for one component, and whole cards are still judged on OGBench.
