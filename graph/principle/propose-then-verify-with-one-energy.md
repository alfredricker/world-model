The user's design of 2026-11-19: recurrence, each output updating the state for the next. Each step the state and the wanted effect are clamped and the action settled; the effect is then settled forward from that action and compared with what was wanted; if it does not come out, the same is asked as if the object were touched, and touching becomes the wanted effect, met from stored touching places. Between steps the agent carries a belief of what it holds, settled forward from what it saw. First tested in [card 149](../../experiments/energy-modules/149-a-sequence-of-actions-by-recurrence/card.md) on [card 148](../../experiments/energy-modules/148-the-action-as-an-output-token/card.md)'s couplings. The energy can only be trusted about combinations its records hold; settling can propose one they do not.
