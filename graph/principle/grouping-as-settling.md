Each patch holds a belief over at most K objects; pairs of patches attract or repel through couplings, and descending the energy with falling temperature gives the grouping, with K a ceiling and not the count. The energy is the Hopfield-Tank form that CRAM learns, with patches as cells and object identities as values. Proposed in [the write-up](../../theory/thousand_brains_energy_formulation.md), section 7; first tested in cards [146](../../experiments/energy-modules/146-grouping-as-settling-on-a-known-benchmark/card.md) and [146.1](../../experiments/energy-modules/146.1-grouping-as-settling-colder/card.md) on features computed by a trained AKOrN. The couplings are not yet learned.
