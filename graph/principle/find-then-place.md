Which patches hold an object is read from their features; where it is comes from the centre of those patches in the image through a low-order map that never sees the features, one per object or one for all ([card 142](../../experiments/142-find-then-place/card.md)). Fitted after the score, it reads 1.84 cm in a withheld third of the table where the offset form reads 3.32, and 1.05 cm inside the range where the offset form reads 0.45. With a shift bounded to one patch added and the weights frozen, it reads 1.21 cm in the withheld third and 0.61 cm inside ([card 144](../../experiments/144-a-centre-that-means-the-middle/card.md)).
