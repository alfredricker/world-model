A read-out fitted on recall's own held-out errors turns its answer, together with how fresh the records in the key are (seen at all, seen in this episode, time since seen), into the stated probability. In [card 139](../../experiments/139-an-answer-that-knows-when-not-to-trust-itself/card.md) it moves cold log loss toward the base rate on every child, but misses three of five bars, so the principle is tested and not adopted. The biological counterpart is the feeling of knowing: people judge whether their memory is likely to be right, and that judgement is learned from how retrieval went before (metacognition; Fleming and Dolan 2012; confidence signals in orbitofrontal cortex, Kepecs and others 2008).
