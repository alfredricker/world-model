The user's design of 2026-11-20: energy modules in layers, every module of a layer updating in parallel, deeper layers reading the layers below continuously; feed-forward first. A module is a memory of stored patterns; it stores its input as a new pattern when nothing fits. Tested in [card 150](../../experiments/energy-modules/150-three-feed-forward-layers-of-energy-modules/card.md) on sprite pixels (motion, binding and object modules) and in [card 151](../../experiments/energy-modules/151-layers-of-attractor-modules-in-a-world-of-parts/card.md) in a world of parts up to an output token. What goes up must be the module's energy for every stored pattern (a belief), which the layer above adds; the winner alone loses most of the answer under noise and the scalar energy alone carries none. Not yet tested: feedback, modules wired by binding instead of a given grid, actions at the top.
