Before acting on a remembered value, the agent looks at the condition object when stored re-sightings say a memory of that age is too often wrong, and always looks when the object was never seen. In [card 135](../../experiments/135-state-that-changes-out-of-sight/card.md) this recovers 74% of the loss from hidden changes for 38% of the looks, but age alone is a weak sign among locks already seen. The biological counterpart is information seeking under uncertainty (Gottlieb and Oudeyer 2018): animals and people look again before acting on an old memory.
