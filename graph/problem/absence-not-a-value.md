title: A target that is not in view is read as being somewhere

---
The agent's reading always returns a position. A cube inside the drawer is read as a point some 45 cm off, and a goal frame without the cube cannot be understood as "put it out of view" ([card 131](../../experiments/131-belief-out-of-view/card.md), data check). This is a fault of the reading and of what a goal may ask for, not of memory: with perfect perception and the camera's view, the cube's absence alone tells a full reopened drawer from an empty one at 100.0% where chance is 50% ([card 132](../../experiments/132-memory-test-bed/card.md)).
